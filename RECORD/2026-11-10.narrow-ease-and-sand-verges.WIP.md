# 2026-11-10 — A narrowing's own ease, and verges of sand by a beach

On `ccr-e7021c37-ulgd1v`.

## User request

For the game's Costa Bits: the road under its overhang of rock down to a
single lane, and no green along the road where it runs by a beach.

## Context

- A `Narrowing`'s walls closed in over `NARROW_EASE`, 25 m, whatever they
  closed in to: from 11.5 m to less than 5 m they step in more than 0.8 m
  every 2 m, which `walls_close_in_on_a_narrow_bridge_and_open_out_after_it`
  refuses.
- On a circuit drawn as voxels the verges beside the road are flat strips in
  two greens, whatever the ground beside them is: green along a beach.

## Implementation

- `src/track.rs`: `Narrowing::ease`, how far before it the walls start closing
  in and after it they have opened out (m); `NARROW_EASE` where a circuit's
  file leaves it out, so every file as it was reads as before. The lap's walls
  and a branch's use it; the check reads each narrowing's own.
- `src/world/terrain.rs` (`verge_colors`): a verge beside a road lower than
  `BEACH_HEIGHT` (where the ground is sand) is sand, top and edge, in the
  bands' two lights; not indoors.
