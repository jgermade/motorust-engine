# 2026-11-03 — The ground as voxels (`look: Voxel`)

## User request

After dropping the pixel filter, the user asked for a prototype of the ground
in voxels on Costa del Sol (a game's circuit). Along the way they asked:

- that the voxels be small, to simulate pixel art (0.5 m seemed big);
- to play with textures that look like pixel art;
- for the pixels to move with the ground, as part of it, not stand still on
  the screen as the filter's did.

## Implementation

- `src/world/scenery.rs`: `Look` is `Smooth` (the default) or
  `Voxel { size, steps, shades }`.
  - `size`: the voxels' size, in metres.
  - `steps`, optional: the ground's shape in blocks too.
  - `shades`, optional: a palette of that many shades of each of red, green
    and blue.
  - `voxel_size`, `voxel_steps` and `voxel_shades` keep them to their limits:
    at least 0.05 m, at least 0.25 m, and 2 to 256 shades.
- **First try, in the geometry** (`steps`, `src/world/terrain.rs`,
  `Terrain::voxel_band` and `block_top`):
  - each cell of the ground's grid becomes a column with a level top, at its
    lowest corner down to a whole number of steps, so it never stands over
    the ground or the road;
  - sheer faces of rock join it to the cells east and south of it;
  - its colour is the smooth ground's at its middle.
  - Seen on screen with steps of 1.75 m, the blocks were as big as the grid's
    cells (3.5 m, more far off), too big. It stays as an option.
- **The voxels, in a shader** (`src/world/voxel.rs`, `src/world/voxel.wgsl`,
  `VoxelPlugin`):
  - The pieces of the ground of a circuit drawn as voxels get, once in the
    world, `VoxelMaterial`, their own standard material extended
    (`voxelize_the_ground`).
  - Where the lawn was drawn stochastically in a twin of the piece
    (`lawn::Lawn`), the twin goes. That twin is why the first version showed
    nothing.
  - Each piece knows its texture's tile (`terrain::GroundTile`, from
    `tile_of`, which `meshes_as` now uses too).
  - The shader, at every point of the ground:
    - It finds the cube of a staircase of cubes `size` metres on a side that
      the ground would show there. A tread where the step at the column's
      middle (from the slope) is this point's, a riser facing down the slope
      otherwise, so cliffs are all risers.
    - It reads the texture once, at the cube's middle, and makes the cube a
      little lighter or darker than the next, the risers darker.
    - It cuts the colour to `shades` (in sRGB) and lights it as the cube's
      face.
  - The cubes are anchored to the world, so they move with the ground. No
    triangle is added, and the outline against the sky stays smooth.

- `cargo +stable fmt --all --check` and
  `cargo +stable clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`: clean.
- `cargo +stable test`: 645 passed, 0 failed, 2 ignored. New tests:
  - `a_circuit_s_look_reads_from_its_file`
  - `in_blocks_the_ground_is_level_tops_and_sheer_faces_never_over_it`
  - `only_a_circuit_drawn_as_voxels_has_its_ground_read_as_voxels`
  - `the_ground_of_a_circuit_drawn_as_voxels_is_given_their_material`
- On screen (the game built against this clone, lavapipe under Xvfb, a
  temporary harness racing on Costa del Sol from a given distance):
  - `Voxel(size: 0.5)`: the grass beside the road is flat-coloured cubes. The
    shade between cubes (±8 %) was too faint to see; it is now ±12 %, with
    risers 10 % darker.
  - `size` 0.25 and 0.15 with `shades: Some(12)`: near the road it reads as
    pixel art. Far off, on the hills, the cubes are smaller than a pixel and
    the rock turns to grain that will shimmer as the camera moves.
- Not yet done: voxels that grow with distance (powers of two), so that each

# 2026-11-04 — The ground as voxels, in blocks of geometry

## User request

Of the shader's voxels, the grass went the right way, but the ground looked very
bad: its pixels did not show, and the far ground made a lot of moiré. The user
asked for real voxels for the ground, and agreed to doing the ground first and
the trees after.

## Implementation

- The shader, its material and `GroundTile` are gone, and so are the first
  try's columns per cell of the grid (`steps`, `voxel_band`). `Look::Voxel` is
  now `{ size, shades }`.
- `src/world/terrain.rs`, `VoxelGrid`, made with the terrain from its track:
  - The fine part of the grid is cut into square tiles of `TILE_BLOCKS` (8)
    of the smallest blocks.
  - Each tile's blocks are 1, 2, 4 or 8 times `size`, by how far its nearest
    corner is from the nearest road's middle (`VOXEL_RINGS`: 35, 90 and
    250 m). The middles are kept in cells of a coarse grid to find the
    nearest quickly.
- `Terrain::voxel_row`, one row of tiles at a time, on every core:
  - Each block is a column. Its top (`voxel_top`) is the lowest of its
    corners' and middle's heights, down to a whole number of its blocks, so
    it never stands over the smooth ground or the road.
  - It has a level top and, on each side, a face where the ground next to it
    stands lower. A side is cut into stretches as long as the smaller of its
    blocks and the neighbour's, each down to that neighbour's top, so no gap
    opens where blocks of two sizes meet.
  - Colours (`voxel_colors`): the smooth ground's tint at the nearest point
    of the grid, times the average colour of that ground's texture. A block
    is ±10 % lighter or darker than the next, and cut to `shades` in sRGB.
    The sides under grass are earth; under the rest, the ground's colour
    darker.
- The smooth ground's cells under the blocks are left out (`band`'s
  `blocks`); past the grid the ground stays smooth.
- The blocks are one mesh, `GroundMesh::plain`, drawn with a material without
  a texture.
- Without a texture, the vertex colours are only tints, and the blocks came
  out pink and lilac. So `WorldTextures::ground_colors` now has the average
  colours of the grass, rock, granite, sand, chequered earth and floor
  textures. `Terrain::paint_blocks` gives them to the grid as the circuit is
  prepared (`world::prepare`, `preview::prepare`).

## Verification

- `cargo +stable fmt --all --check` and
  `cargo +stable clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`: clean.
- `cargo +stable test`: 644 passed, 0 failed, 2 ignored. New tests:
  - `a_circuit_s_look_reads_from_its_file`
  - `the_blocks_are_smallest_by_the_road_and_grow_away_from_it`
  - `as_voxels_the_ground_is_level_tops_and_sheer_sides_never_over_it`: every
    vertex at a whole number of blocks; tops never over the smooth ground;
    sides level and facing across; no smooth ground under the blocks.
- On screen (Costa del Sol, `Voxel(size: 1.0, shades: Some(12))`, lavapipe):
  - The cliffs and hillsides are walls of block columns.
  - The far hills are terraces of blocks, with no moiré.
  - The colours are right once painted with the textures' averages.
  - Not in blocks yet: the grass verges by the road (their own mesh) and the
    trees.

# 2026-11-04 — Trees as flat sprites of pixel art

## User request

The trees were to be flat: "photographed" at a low resolution from the 3D
ones for now; by how they come out, the user will decide whether to ask
Meshy for them.

## Implementation

- `src/world/sprites.rs` (`SpritesPlugin`):
  - `photograph`: a small CPU rasterizer of a mesh's triangles, by their
    vertices' colours, with a depth buffer. It sees the mesh from in front,
    `pixel` metres a pixel, the foot at the bottom middle, and lights each
    triangle as it faces the sun (from in front, above and the left). The
    border with the see-through pixels is outlined 45 % darker, and the
    colours are cut to `shades` in sRGB. The image is sampled nearest.
  - `card`: an upright quad, its foot at the origin.
  - `card_material`: unlit, alpha masked, both sides.
  - `FacingCamera` and `face_the_camera`: in `PostUpdate`, before the
    transforms propagate, turn every card about its upright to face the first
    active 3D camera (`facing`).
- `src/world/props.rs`:
  - On a circuit drawn as voxels, `plan_trees` photographs each variant of
    each species (its classic mesh, painted in its vertices) at
    `SPRITE_PIXELS` (4) pixels for each metre of the smallest blocks. This
    runs off the main thread, with the rest of the circuit's preparation.
  - `spawn_trees` plants them as cards, at their scale, casting no shadow
    (it would turn with them), and with no `Tree`, so the options' dressing
    leaves them alone. The sea still mirrors their crowns.

## Verification

- `cargo +stable fmt --all --check` and
  `cargo +stable clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`: clean.
- `cargo +stable test`: 646 passed, 0 failed, 2 ignored. New tests:
  - `a_thing_is_photographed_its_foot_at_the_bottom_middle_outlined_and_cut_to_a_palette`
  - `a_card_faces_the_camera_about_its_upright`
- On screen (Costa del Sol):
  - The forest over the headland is flat firs of pixel art.
  - At the fork, umbrella pines, cypresses and almond trees in flower.
  - Not yet sprites: the palms (their own rows, `spawn_palms`) and the houses.

# 2026-11-04 — An arcade road, walls, palms and light for the voxels

## User request

The user did not like the road's shoulders. On screenshots of the arcade
racers of the 8 and 16 bits, they asked for the road and what is beside it to
take their look, and then:

- for the walls to change too;
- for the palms to become flat as well;
- for the light to be used so that the voxels stand out more.

The genre's conventions are what is taken, not any one game's art.

## Implementation

- `src/world/road.rs`, on a circuit drawn as voxels (not indoors):
  - **Bands:** `ARCADE_BAND` (6 m) and `arcade_band`.
  - **Asphalt:** flat, in bands of two greys (`ARCADE_ASPHALT`), with no
    texture.
  - **Curbs:** all the way round, not only in the bends, striped band by band
    in the circuit's two colours. Only drawn: the curbs' physics, where the
    track is paved, is as it was.
  - **Walls:** stone, concrete, parapets, hedges and timber are drawn in their
    colours alone (`arcade_wall_color`), two shades band by band and a
    lighter coping on the stone. Their foot reaches down `ARCADE_FOOTING`
    (6 m) to the blocks, where it used to leave a gap under them.
- `src/world/props.rs`: the palms of the rows are photographed as sprites
  (three variants) and planted as cards facing the camera, as the trees are.
- `src/world/terrain.rs`:
  - **Banded grass:** `VoxelGrid` keeps a map, a cell per smallest block, of
    the band of the nearest road within `BANDED_REACH` (30 m). It is drawn by
    sweeping across every road at each point of its middle. The grass blocks
    there are in two greens band by band, each block hardly lighter or darker
    than the next.
  - **Light:** the blocks' sides are shaded as a voxel world's, whatever the
    sun (`side_light`: east 0.82, south 0.74, west 0.62, north 0.56 against
    the tops), and darker towards their foot (`VOXEL_FOOT`, 0.5), as an
    ambient occlusion, so that every step stands out.
- `src/world/mod.rs`: no tufts nor ragged edge of grass on a circuit drawn as
  voxels.

## Verification

- `cargo +stable fmt --all --check` and
  `cargo +stable clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`: clean.
- `cargo +stable test`: 646 passed, 0 failed, 2 ignored.
- On screen (Costa del Sol, now with `sky: Dusk`):
  - The road is banded and the curbs are striped all the way.
  - The palms are flat.
  - The cliffs' steps stand out by their shaded sides.

# 2026-11-04 — A retro sunset sky (`SkyStyle::Synthwave`)

## User request

A dusk light with the sun's reflection on the sea, in the style of the
synthwave pictures the user sent: magenta and indigo, a huge striped sun.
Then: the sun cannot stand in front of the clouds.

## Implementation

- `src/world/scenery.rs`: `SkyStyle::Synthwave`.
- `src/world/sky.rs`:
  - **Its palette:** indigo overhead, magenta down to the horizon, orange and
    yellow only round the sun (so that the water mirrors magenta, dark, and
    the sun's light shows on it). The sun is low (0.07 rad) in the
    west-south-west (1.32 π). Pink light, violet shade, magenta clouds, a
    grade 1.15 saturated and 1.08 contrasted.
  - **Its sun:** `synthwave_sun_image`, 256 px, a disc from yellow at its top
    to orange and magenta at its bottom, its lower half cut by stripes that
    thicken downwards, with a soft glow. It is drawn `SYNTHWAVE_SUN` (2.6)
    times as big as the others' sun, in its own colours.
  - **The glint:** `Palette::glint`, the power and the brightness of the
    sun's glint on the water. The other skies keep theirs as it was
    (`GLINT`, 600 and 4). The retro sunset's is a column under the sun (220,
    2.5). At 24 and 3 it whitened the whole sea.
  - **Behind the clouds:** the sun's disc is now at `SUN_DISTANCE`, 6 % past
    the clouds and inside the dome, and 6 % bigger to look as big. At the
    clouds' own distance, and so much bigger, it was drawn in front of them.
- `src/world/mirror.rs` and `src/world/mirror.wgsl`: `MirrorLook::under(palette,
  deep)` puts the glint's power in `deep.w`, unused until now, and its
  brightness in `sun.w`. The shader raises to that power.

## Verification

- `cargo +stable fmt --all --check` and
  `cargo +stable clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`: clean.
- `cargo +stable test`: 648 passed, 0 failed, 2 ignored. New tests:
  - `the_retro_sunset_is_a_huge_striped_sun_low_over_the_sea_in_magenta_and_indigo`
  - `the_sun_is_behind_the_clouds_and_inside_the_sky`
- On screen (Costa del Sol's coast highway, heading west):
  - The striped sun is ahead and to the left over the sea, with a bright
    band of its light on the water below it.
  - A cloud covers the top of the sun.

# 2026-11-04 — The retro sunset brighter, its water violet

## User request

On Costa del Sol everything looked too red and too dark, with too many very
dark stretches of road, and the water looked green.

## Implementation

`src/world/sky.rs`, `SkyStyle::Synthwave`:

- **Sunlight:** paler (1.0, 0.82, 0.76) and stronger (1.1).
- **The sun:** higher (0.16 rad), so the shadows across the road are shorter.
  At 0.07 rad they darkened whole stretches of it.
- **The shade:** more and paler light in it (`ambient`: 0.78, 0.7, 1.0 at 1100).
- **The grade:** saturation 1.05 instead of 1.15.
- **The water:** `Palette::deep_water`, a sky's own colour of deep water.
  Under this sky it is violet (0.2, 0.08, 0.32); `MirrorLook::under` puts it
  in place of the circuit's sea, which was the teal that made it green. The
  other skies have none.

## Verification

  `cargo +stable clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`: clean.
- `cargo +stable test`: 655 passed, 0 failed, 2 ignored. That includes the

# 2026-11-04 — Flat verges in bands of green; a softer retro sky

## User request

On Costa del Sol's mountain road, the blocks between the curbs and the walls
rose beside the road in stairs as it climbed. They should go, and the curbs'
red and white stripes carry on there, in greens. Also, the sky's purple was
too purple: softer.

## Implementation

- `src/world/terrain.rs`:
  - `Verge`, and `VoxelGrid::verges` (made by `verges`; `VoxelGrid::new`
    now takes the scenery): the road's surface carried on flat past its
    curbs, out to `FLAT` (as far as the ground is the road's own). It is in
    strips a segment long and up to `VERGE_STRIP` (1 m) across, banded as
    the road (`arcade_band`), just under the curbs (`VERGE_UNDER`). Its
    outer edge drops `VERGE_DROP` (3 m) to the blocks, in their earth and
    side light. Not on the lap's bridges or in its tunnels (decks and
    walkways), nor where another road, or another stretch of the same one,
    is nearer.
  - `Terrain::verge_colors`: the banded grass of the blocks beside the road,
    level, never weathered and of one shade all across (the grass of the
    road's middle beside it), so that the bands go by unbroken. The two
    bands of green further apart (`ARCADE_GREENS`, from 1.12 and 0.9 to 1.22
    and 0.8), there and on the blocks, to tell them apart.
    `Terrain::verge_mesh`: a second plain mesh beside the blocks'.
  - `VoxelGrid::cut` (the colours cut to the grid's shades), `voxel_quad`
    and `VOXEL_EARTH`, taken out of the blocks' code to share them.
  - The blocks under a verge stay, hidden: they lie at least 0.07 m below it.
- `src/world/sky.rs`: the synthwave zenith from (0.09, 0.03, 0.28) to a
  dusky lavender (0.27, 0.21, 0.47), and above the horizon from
  (0.62, 0.12, 0.5) to a mauve (0.8, 0.4, 0.6).

## Verification

- `cargo +stable clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`: clean.
- `cargo +stable test`: 656 passed, 0 failed, 2 ignored; among them
  `terrain::tests`, 20, one of them new,
  `as_voxels_the_verges_carry_the_road_on_flat_in_its_bands_with_no_steps`.
- Seen in the game (lavapipe, Costa del Sol from 2150 m and 600 m): the
  verge runs flat to the walls with no stairs, its bands of green in step
  with the curbs' stripes; the sky lavender and mauve. With the colour of
