# 2026-11-04 — A music player in Options > Sound

On `ccr-5ceb056d-y3evh6`.

## User request

The songs no track uses are kept, not removed. Options gets a music player
that lists every song, saying which are a circuit's and which are loose.

## Implementation

- `src/sound/music.rs`:
  - `Songs::loose` (`#[serde(default)]`): songs no track or screen plays,
    named in `music.ron` as the chill ones are.
  - `Tune::Loose`, played at a track's loudness.
  - `Tune::title`: a track's song's `title`, or its file's name in words
    (`title_of`: "Crown of the Track"). The others use their names.
  - `Tune::played_for`: the track, "Menús"/"Menus", "Chill" or
    "Suelta"/"Loose".
  - `player_tunes`, in the player's order: the tracks' songs (a file two
    tracks share, once), the menus', the chill ones, then the loose ones.
  - `Jukebox` (a resource, registered by `SoundPlugin`): the song the
    player plays. `play_music` plays it in place of the screen's, whatever
    the screen, at full fader (not lowered by the pause menu), and even
    with the music off, at the music's volume.
- `src/circuits/`: `Song::title`, an optional `title` in a song's file.
- `src/options.rs`:
  - `SoundItem::Player`, a row of its own group at Sound's foot, which
    opens `Page::Player` ("REPRODUCTOR" / "MUSIC PLAYER");
  - on the page, a `SoundItem::Tune` for each song: its title, and what it
    is played for. A (or Enter) plays it or, playing, stops it; the one
    playing is in amber, "· sonando" / "· playing".
  - `stop_the_player` stops it once the page is left or the options close.
  - Reset has nothing to put back there.
  - `sound_rows` draws Sound's rows. `options_count` and `tab_items_count`
    take how many songs the player has.
  - `the_music_stays_well_under_the_player_s_engine_on` leaves the loose
    songs out: only the player plays them, never over a race's engines.
    One of the game's (heavy in the bass) was 0.7 dB over that margin.
