# The Ground Drawn as Voxels, in Squares, Indexed and Merged

## 1. The Voxel Ground Cut into Squares, Its Quads Indexed, Its Tops Merged (2026-11-23)

**Date:** 2026-11-23. **Branch:** `claude/epic-darwin-64c8v7`, from `main` at `1186815` (v0.0.26). Worked on with the game beside it under a `[patch]`.

### 1.1 User Request
- On a base M4 Mac mini a game's voxel circuit (Costa Bits) ran at 45 fps with one player and 23 with two at full detail, against 115–200 on its other circuits. The benchmark said it was neither the CPU (the main world's update 2.2 ms, 7 % of the cores) nor the pixels (the render scale at 50 % or no antialiasing changed almost nothing), but the ground's shadows: without the ground in them it ran at 111 fps. The user asked to start with the voxel ground's mesh: "sí, empieza por ahí".

### 1.2 What Was Found
- The blocks were one mesh over the whole circuit (`Terrain::meshes_as`), plus one for the verges: never culled by the cameras nor by the sun's cascades (four at high, up to 250 m), so all of it was drawn in the main pass and in every cascade, and again for each view with two players.
- On that circuit (blocks of 1 m at their smallest, 12 shades a channel): 4.72 M vertices and 1.57 M triangles, against well under 1 M vertices for the whole ground of any smooth circuit. Each quad had six unshared vertices, with texture coordinates always zero.
- The loop adding tangents to what is drawn with relief (`world/mod.rs`) took the blocks for grass and gave them tangents too: another 16 bytes for each of the millions of vertices, for a material with no texture.

### 1.3 Implementation Details
All in `src/world/terrain.rs` but for the two callers:
- **Squares.** `VoxelGrid::chunks` splits the grid's tiles into squares of about `CHUNK` (128 m) a side, a whole number of tiles; `Terrain::voxel_chunk` (was `voxel_row`) builds one square, and `meshes_as` builds them on the cores, dealt out in turn as the bands are, each its own `GroundMesh`; squares with nothing in them are left out. The verges are put in the square their middle lies in (`VoxelGrid::chunk_at`), each square of them a mesh (`Terrain::verge_meshes`). Each square is its own entity, so Bevy culls it by its bounds against the cameras and each cascade. `spawn_terrain` gives them all one material (it made one a mesh before), so they can be batched.
- **`GroundMesh::plain` is now `Option<Plain>`** (`Plain::Blocks` or `Plain::Verges`), so the blocks' squares and the verges' can be told apart (`structures.rs` sets `None`).
- **Indexed quads.** `Builder::quad` adds four vertices and six indices; `voxel_quad` uses it. `Builder::build_plain` builds those meshes with no texture coordinates (`Builder::empty_mesh` holds the asset usage both share).
- **Merged tops** (`merge_tops`, greedy meshing). Each square's tops are gathered as `Top` (block size, cell in blocks, height, colour) and merged: sorted by size, then by row and column, each top not yet taken grown along its row while its neighbours are of the same size, height and colour, then down while the whole run of the next row is too. A merged quad's corners are corners of its blocks, so no top lies over the smooth ground. The sides are left as they were. The blocks' colour jitter (each block a little lighter or darker than the next, the look of the voxels) limits the merging: the tops go from 612,000 to 324,000 quads.
- **No tangents for the plain meshes**: the relief loop in `world/mod.rs` skips them.

On that circuit: 249 squares, 2.10 M vertices (from 4.72 M, −55 %) and 1.05 M triangles (from 1.57 M), the mesh built in about 120 ms on eight threads (143 ms before).

### 1.4 Verification & Testing
- New test `the_blocks_tops_merge_where_side_by_side_at_one_height_and_colour`: a 4 × 4 of the same tops is one quad over them, two heights two quads, a checkerboard of two colours none merged, two sizes never merged.
- `as_voxels_the_ground_is_level_tops_and_sheer_sides_never_over_it` now checks the squares: more than one, each no wider than a square, no texture coordinates, four vertices a quad. `as_voxels_the_verges_carry_the_road_on_flat_in_its_bands_with_no_steps` reads the verges' squares.
- `cargo test`: 692 passed, 3 ignored. `cargo fmt --all --check` and `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean. The game's `cargo test` (255 passed), fmt and clippy clean with this engine under a `[patch]`.
- **Benchmark** on the base M4 Mac mini (macOS 27.2.1, 1920 × 1080, MSAA 4×, high shadows), the voxel circuit alone, against the same machine's run of the same day before the change:

  | scenario | before | after |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1p | 45.5 fps | 157.2 fps |
  | 1p, no ground shadows | 111.1 | 176.9 |
  | 1p, shadows low | 68.5 | 185.3 |
  | 2p | 36.0 | 121.2 |
  | 2p, full detail | 23.3 | 83.1 |

  The process's memory at most 2.7 GiB, from 3.8. The ground's shadows now cost 12 %, not 144 %: taking the far blocks out of the shadows, or the rings of block sizes made narrower, is not needed for now.
