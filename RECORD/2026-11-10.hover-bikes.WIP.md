# 2026-11-10 — Hover bikes: floating, bobbing, and a drift on the wheelie's button

Branch `claude/youthful-davinci-0c70wu` (the game's branch of the same name
has the models and the circuit that use it).

## User request

The user gave three hover bikes made with Meshy (bikes with pads where the
wheels would be) and asked for a circuit in homage to the anti-gravity
racers. Then, as the work went on:

- "The hover bikes float; stopped, they will bob a little up and down."
- "The wheelie's button, instead of a wheelie, on the hover bikes is used to
  drift in the bends."

## Implementation

- **A circuit says its bikes hover**: `Circuit::hover` (`src/circuits/mod.rs`,
  `hover: true` in its file; `false` if left out).
- **The roster says what each bike is there**: `Entry::hover`
  (`hover: Some(Model("folder"))`, none if left out), `Entry::design_on`. A
  roster is refused whose `hover` is not a model that hovers, or whose
  `design` is one. `BikeAssets::spawn` takes whether the bikes hover; the
  grid (`spawn_grid`) and the bike select screen's showcase
  (`menu::spawn_showcase`) pass the circuit's.
- **A model says it hovers** (`src/bike/model/imported.rs`, `Model`):
  - `hover: Some((glow: (r, g, b)))`: no wheels (its `bike.bin` is its
    `chassis` alone, modelled floating); its axles are the middles of its
    pads and their radii the pads';
  - `rider: Some("folder")`: it carries another model's rider (`pilot.bin`
    and `pilot_color.jpg` read from there); each rider is loaded once
    whichever models carry them (`BikeBlueprint`);
  - `emissive: true`: its own lights, `emissive.jpg` in its folder.
- **`src/bike/model/hover.rs`** (new): `Hover` (what `model.ron` says),
  `Hovering` (on the bike), `bob` (how far it floats above its rest height:
  a slow swing of 4.5 cm standing still, 1.2 cm flat out, each bike at its
  own point of it), and the pools of light on the road under the pads
  (`PadGlow`: a flat square with a round falloff, added to what is under it,
  hidden in the air and after a fall). `move_chassis` adds the bob to the
  body's lift; `pose_pilots` keeps the rider's foot on its peg when stopped.
- **The drift** (`src/bike/mod.rs`, `step`): `BikeTuning::hover`, kept as the
  circuit chosen says (`follow_circuit`). With it:
  - what pulls a wheelie pulls none (`Forces::pull` is 0);
  - held into a bend over 12 m/s (all of it from 30 m/s), `BikeState::drift`
    swings towards `DRIFT_ANGLE` (0.42 rad) the way the bike steers, and back
    when let go, when the steering straightens or in the air;
  - drifting all out, the bike turns 30 % tighter where its grip limits it
    (`DRIFT_TURN`), is spared 70 % of what holding the steering at its limit
    costs (`DRIFT_SPARES`: the engine's cut and `CORNER_SCRUB`), and pays
    5 m/s² for sliding (`DRIFT_DRAG`): quicker through a tight bend, slower
    through a gentle one;
  - stopped, it stands level (`parked_lean`, `BikeState::standing`).
  `interpolate_bikes` turns the lean pivot by the drift, so the bike and its
  rider point into the bend. The rivals never pull a wheelie, so they never
  drift.
- **What tells the player**: the race legend names the key "Drift" /
  "Derrape" where the bikes hover (`legend::race_prompts`,
  `Language::action_drift`); the engine's wheelie roar follows the drift
  (`sound`).

## 2026-11-10 — The look of an anti-gravity track

### User request

"The paving must not look like asphalt" (with screenshots of anti-gravity
racers as examples: plates, dark panels, bands of amber, barriers with
chevrons and strips of light). The engine's are of its own design.

### Implementation

- `Scenery::paving` (`Paving::Asphalt`, the default, or `Plates`,
  `src/world/scenery.rs`): a road of plates (`src/world/road.rs`,
  `spawn_surface`): plates in two greys, dark panels let into them either
  side of a spine, a band of amber across every 60 m, a strip of light
  along each edge in the circuit's first curb colour (`light_strip`); no
  painted lines, no skid marks.
- `WallStyle::Barrier`: a race track's barrier of dark panels, its skirt
  banded in amber, a strip of light along its top.
- `BridgeStyle::Skyway`: a dark deck on pairs of pylons, nothing overhead,
  between such barriers (`structures::skyway_meshes`).
- `SkyStyle::Night`: the space sky's stars, Milky Way and Moon over the
  Earth, the light the Moon's, cold and faint, the shade filled enough to
  race by.

### Verification

- `cargo test`: 673 passed, 0 failed, 2 ignored (the count given above,
  675, took the two ignored for passed). `cargo fmt --all --check` and
  `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`: clean.
- Seen in the game under Xvfb: the plates, the strips of light and the
  night sky. The new pieces have no tests of their own yet beyond the
  circuits' checks the game runs on them.

Still to do: tests of the paving and the barrier, speed pads, tubes of

## 2026-11-10 — The drift charges a boost

### User request

"Does the drift behave as Mario Kart's? I think it gains a boost by how
long it has drifted."

### Implementation

`src/bike/mod.rs`, `step`: `BikeState::charge` adds up the time a hover bike
slides well (over 60 % of its drift), to 3 s at most; when the button is let
go after 0.6 s or more of it, `BikeState::boost` takes half a second for each
second charged, during which the bike is driven 14 m/s² harder and may go
10 % past its top speed. Let go sooner there is none; in the air the charge
is lost; on wheels neither ever moves.

### Verification

- `a_drift_held_charges_a_boost_given_when_it_is_let_go`; the bike's tests,
  202 passed. `cargo fmt` and `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`:
  clean. The game's tests against it: 235 passed, 0 failed, 1 ignored.
- Not yet shown to the player (no light or sound says the charge is ready).

## 2026-11-10 — A race track's tunnel

### User request

"The tunnel is the same as in every circuit: how can we change it?"

### Implementation

`src/world/structures.rs`, `structure_pieces`: under a road of plates
(`Paving::Plates`) a tunnel is a race track's: its lining dark panels, its
frame dark, its lamps, their strips and the pools of their light in the
circuit's first curb colour, and a ring of that light round the road every
16 m, up each wall and across under the roof (`tunnel_rings`).

### Verification

- `cargo test`: 674 passed, 0 failed, 2 ignored; `cargo fmt` and `cargo
  clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean.
- Seen in the game under Xvfb: dark, cyan-lit, a ring ahead. `tunnel_rings`
  has no test of its own yet.
