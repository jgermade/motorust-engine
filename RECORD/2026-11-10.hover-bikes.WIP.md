# 2026-11-10 — Hover bikes: floating, bobbing, and a drift on the wheelie's button

Branch `claude/youthful-davinci-0c70wu` (the game's branch of the same name
has the models and the circuit that use it).

## User request

The user gave three hover bikes made with Meshy (bikes with pads where the
wheels would be) and asked for a circuit in homage to the anti-gravity
racers. Then, as the work went on:

- "The hover bikes float; stopped, they will bob a little up and down."
- "The wheelie's button, instead of a wheelie, on the hover bikes is used to
  drift in the bends."

## Implementation

- **A circuit says its bikes hover**: `Circuit::hover` (`src/circuits/mod.rs`,
  `hover: true` in its file; `false` if left out).
- **The roster says what each bike is there**: `Entry::hover`
  (`hover: Some(Model("folder"))`, none if left out), `Entry::design_on`. A
  roster is refused whose `hover` is not a model that hovers, or whose
  `design` is one. `BikeAssets::spawn` takes whether the bikes hover; the
  grid (`spawn_grid`) and the bike select screen's showcase
  (`menu::spawn_showcase`) pass the circuit's.
- **A model says it hovers** (`src/bike/model/imported.rs`, `Model`):
  - `hover: Some((glow: (r, g, b)))`: no wheels (its `bike.bin` is its
    `chassis` alone, modelled floating); its axles are the middles of its
    pads and their radii the pads';
  - `rider: Some("folder")`: it carries another model's rider (`pilot.bin`
    and `pilot_color.jpg` read from there); each rider is loaded once
    whichever models carry them (`BikeBlueprint`);
  - `emissive: true`: its own lights, `emissive.jpg` in its folder.
- **`src/bike/model/hover.rs`** (new): `Hover` (what `model.ron` says),
  `Hovering` (on the bike), `bob` (how far it floats above its rest height:
  a slow swing of 4.5 cm standing still, 1.2 cm flat out, each bike at its
  own point of it), and the pools of light on the road under the pads
  (`PadGlow`: a flat square with a round falloff, added to what is under it,
  hidden in the air and after a fall). `move_chassis` adds the bob to the
  body's lift; `pose_pilots` keeps the rider's foot on its peg when stopped.
- **The drift** (`src/bike/mod.rs`, `step`): `BikeTuning::hover`, kept as the
  circuit chosen says (`follow_circuit`). With it:
  - what pulls a wheelie pulls none (`Forces::pull` is 0);
  - held into a bend over 12 m/s (all of it from 30 m/s), `BikeState::drift`
    swings towards `DRIFT_ANGLE` (0.42 rad) the way the bike steers, and back
    when let go, when the steering straightens or in the air;
  - drifting all out, the bike turns 30 % tighter where its grip limits it
    (`DRIFT_TURN`), is spared 70 % of what holding the steering at its limit
    costs (`DRIFT_SPARES`: the engine's cut and `CORNER_SCRUB`), and pays
    5 m/s² for sliding (`DRIFT_DRAG`): quicker through a tight bend, slower
    through a gentle one;
  - stopped, it stands level (`parked_lean`, `BikeState::standing`).
  `interpolate_bikes` turns the lean pivot by the drift, so the bike and its
  rider point into the bend. The rivals never pull a wheelie, so they never
  drift.
- **What tells the player**: the race legend names the key "Drift" /
  "Derrape" where the bikes hover (`legend::race_prompts`,
  `Language::action_drift`); the engine's wheelie roar follows the drift
  (`sound`).

## 2026-11-10 — The drift charges a boost

### User request

"Does the drift behave as Mario Kart's? I think it gains a boost by how
long it has drifted."

### Implementation

`src/bike/mod.rs`, `step`: `BikeState::charge` adds up the time a hover bike
slides well (over 60 % of its drift), to 3 s at most; when the button is let
go after 0.6 s or more of it, `BikeState::boost` takes half a second for each
second charged, during which the bike is driven 14 m/s² harder and may go
10 % past its top speed. Let go sooner there is none; in the air the charge
is lost; on wheels neither ever moves.

### Verification

- `a_drift_held_charges_a_boost_given_when_it_is_let_go`; the bike's tests,
  202 passed. `cargo fmt` and `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`:
  clean. The game's tests against it: 235 passed, 0 failed, 1 ignored.
- Not yet shown to the player (no light or sound says the charge is ready).

## 2026-11-11 — Tubes of glass, a paving that changes, and a city's haze

### User request

- Stretches as tubes of glass, floating, where the bikes can ride round and
  upside down, all the way round; long, a bend and a straight; passing over
  another part of the lap; "the tube seems to have no thickness".
- The paving must not be the same all along ("look at the pictures"), and
  grey rather than brown.
- A look more of "Blade Runner", with no grass; the circuit mostly in the
  air.

### Implementation

- **Tubes** (`src/track.rs`): `Layout::tubes` (`Tube { from, to, radius }`).
  The lap curls up into a tube over 50 m either end (`curl_tubes`: how much
  at each point, `Track::roll_at`; its walls out past the tube's top; no
  banking there). The bikes ride it as a road as wide as the tube is round,
  laid flat: nothing of their riding changes, but past the top of the tube
  a bike is moved to the same place reached round the other side
  (`Track::round_the_tube`, in `advance_hitting_walls`), so it goes all the
  way round. What is drawn is rolled up: `Track::rolled`,
  `Track::surface_point` (so the road, its paint and what follows it curl
  with it; a ribbon across it is cut into strips there, or it would be a
  flat chord over the bikes) and the bikes themselves
  (`Track::rolled_beside`, in `interpolate_bikes`), upside down at the top.
  All round a tube is road (`surface_beside`).
- **`BridgeStyle::Tube`** (`src/world/road.rs`, `tube_meshes`): glass from
  the plates' edges up and over, and a frame standing out of it: a hoop of
  light every 12 m and rails along it, each a beam 0.45 m deep. No deck, no
  pylons, no barriers.
- **A lap may pass over itself on a bridge** as over a tunnel
  (`walls_of_distant_stretches_do_not_meet_on`); walls need not be back
  after a bridge another follows; a circuit with no rock by its roads need
  have no earth inside its walls.
- **`Paving::Plates` in designs** (`plate_design`): every 160 m another of
  four (pale plates with dark panels and a spine; a dark road with bars of
  amber; pale plates with dark chevrons pointing the way; a grey road in
  lanes), in cooler greys.
- **`SkyStyle::Smog`**: a great city's night under its haze: no stars, a
  dull glow of rust low down, dark overhead, the light cool and dim.

### Verification

- `cargo test`: 676 passed, 0 failed, 2 ignored (new: the designs change
  stretch after stretch; a tube's top is half way round it). `cargo fmt`
  and `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`: clean.
- The game's 238 tests pass on a circuit with a tube 720 m long.
- Seen in the game under Xvfb: the road curls up into the tube, its floor
  under the bike, its frame round it; the designs follow one another.
- Not tested yet beyond that: riding round the top (no test rides a bike
  over it), and how the chase camera looks from up there (it stays level,
  behind the bike).

## 2026-11-11 — Faster hover bikes with a turbine's sound, a skyway built on something, a paving in six designs, the camera inside the tube

### User request

After trying it: the circuit is in the air but must be seen to be built on
something; the bikes should be far faster and quicker off the line, and not
sound as engines; the paving is monotonous ("we had agreed to change it by
stretches"); lights along the circuit; in the tube the bike should not
lean, only go round, and the camera be inside.

### Implementation

- **`BikeTuning::hovering`** (`BikeTuning::on(circuit)`, set by
  `follow_circuit`): 100 m/s flat out (360 km/h, the top of the dial)
  against 80, 26 m/s² off the line against 14.5, and as much more grip
  across as its speed needs for the same bends. The circuits' checks of
  the rivals (`src/bike/ai.rs`) ride each circuit with its own bikes; the
  flat-out lap that only steers is still ridden on wheels.
- **A turbine's sound** (`src/sound/synth.rs`, `EngineVoice::turbine`,
  `EngineParams::hover`): three notes a little out of tune climbing with
  the speed, the rush of the thrust louder with the throttle, a roar while
  drifting; no explosions.
- **`BridgeStyle::Skyway`**: at each pair of pylons a mast either side
  rising 13 m over the deck, a beam across their tops, stays from every
  other pair down to the deck's edges, and under each beam a strip of light
  and a lamp (`skyway_lamps`, among the circuit's `Lamps`).
- **`Paving::Plates` in six designs**, every 220 m another: grey plates
  with dark panels; a black road with three lines of light; pale plates
  with dark chevrons; a dark road with amber chevrons; a blue-grey road in
  lanes; a black road with bars of light across (the circuit's second curb
  colour).
- **In a tube**: the bike stands upright on its wall (its lean fades as the
  road curls up, `interpolate_bikes`), and the chase camera goes round with
  it, inside (`follow_player`: the tilt it has round a loop).
- A gantry inside a tube is not drawn (its checkpoint is there all the
  same).

### Verification

- `cargo test`: 677 passed, 0 failed, 2 ignored (new: a bike that hovers
  whines as a turbine, with far less below 120 Hz than an engine). `cargo
  fmt` and `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`: clean.
- The game's tests against it: 239 passed.
- Heard by no one yet: the turbine was only measured. The camera in the
  tube and the upright bike have no test of their own.

## 2026-11-11 — A way off the lap that crosses over it: a braided fork

### User request

"A fork where one goes up and the other down, and they cross, braided",
much longer than the other circuits' forks (with a drawing: the two ways
part, cross 5 m apart, and go on).

### What was wrong

A first try (see the game's record) failed five checks. Traced: where a
way off the lap shares the lap's surface (near its ends), the engine took
the lap's height from the stretch of the lap nearest seen from above. Where
the lap passes over itself there (Puerto Neón's join is right under its
tube), that is the other stretch: a bike on the way was lifted 18 m onto
the tube. Laying the way had the same fault.

### Implementation (`src/track.rs`)

- `Track::project_on_lap_at`: the stretch of the lap at about a height,
  where it passes over itself; used for the height of a way off the lap
  where it shares the lap's surface (`height_beside`, `surface_point_on`),
  at the way's own level.
- Laying a way without a loop (`side_road`), each point is put beside the
  stretch of the lap it counts as (within 220 m of it, `LAP_AROUND`), not
  the nearest stretch seen from above.
- `apart_between_its_ends`: once quite apart from the lap, a way keeps its
  own surface until it comes back to join it, however near it passes on
  the way (crossing over or under it a storey apart).
- `a_fork_leaves_the_lap_and_joins_it_again_past_rock_on`: a way may cross
  the lap where the two are more than 8 m apart in height
  (`CROSSING_APART`); there its side and walls are not the lap's concern,
  and after it, it is on the other side.

### Verification

- `cargo test`: 677 passed, 0 failed, 2 ignored; `cargo fmt` and `cargo
  clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean.
- The game's tests with Puerto Neón's braided fork: 240 passed, 0 failed,
  every other circuit's forks and loops as before.

## 2026-11-11 — The nitro, 540 km/h, the drift on X and the nitro on A, a way off the lap that does not vanish

### User request

After trying it: "the fork is confusing: the road appears and disappears
depending on where you go"; it still feels slow: the hover bikes' top
speed 600 km/h, riding at 540 at their peak and nearing 600 with the
nitro; on the gamepad X (square) drifts and A (cross) is the nitro; the
nitro filled by drifting and on stretches that fill it, as the pink bars.

### Implementation

- **Speed** (`BikeTuning::hovering`): 150 m/s flat out (540 km/h), 40 m/s²
  off the line, its grip across scaled for its speed; a boost (the nitro,
  a pad) 11 % past it, to about 600 km/h (`BOOST_TOP_SPEED`).
- **The nitro** (`src/bike/mod.rs`): `BikeState::nitro`, a tank from 0 to
  1. A drift let go once charged fills it, 0.12 a second charged (up to 3
  s, `NITRO_PER_CHARGE`) instead of a boost of its own; a stretch that
  fills it (`Layout::recharges`, `Recharge { from, to }`,
  `Track::recharge_beside`, `Ground::recharge`) fills 0.35 a second;
  `BikeInput::nitro` held burns 0.25 a second and drives the bike on as a
  boost while there is any. Only where the bikes hover. The rivals burn
  theirs on a straight with 30 % or more.
- **Controls** (`src/settings.rs`): a new action, `Action::Nitro` ("Nitro"),
  on E and Left Ctrl and the gamepad's A (cross); the wheelie (the drift)
  on Space and Left Shift and X (square) and B (circle); the throttle and
  the brake on their triggers alone. Settings saved before have A taken
  from the throttle for the nitro and X moved from the brake to the
  wheelie (`give_the_nitro_its_button`), unless their player remapped
  the nitro. In split screen the nitro is E (WASD), K or Numpad 1
  (arrows), A (gamepad). Where A is the nitro, RT and A together no
  longer pull a wheelie.
- **The speedometer**: for the bikes that hover, the same dial reads to
  600 km/h every 50 (360 mph every 30); a bar of the nitro under the
  unit; the race legend names the nitro where the bikes hover.
- **The stretches that fill the nitro** are drawn on a road of plates as
  bars of light across it in the second curb colour (pink at Puerto
  Neón), and the paving's designs no longer use those bars.
- **A way off the lap that crosses it no longer vanishes**: what of a
  road is left out where another road's asphalt covers it (its asphalt,
  its paint, its walls where they meet) took the other road seen from
  above; now only another road at its level counts
  (`Track::on_other_road_at`), so a way over the lap is drawn whole.
- **A way off the lap where the lap is a skyway is one too**: its deck,
  pylons, masts and lamps where it is apart from the lap
  (`Track::apart_at`).

### Verification

- `cargo test`: 682 passed, 0 failed, 2 ignored (new: the drift fills the
  nitro, a stretch fills it, it drives the bike on and burns, none on
  wheels; the nitro on A and the drift on X, in settings saved before
  too; the dial to 600 km/h; the bars only where the nitro fills).
  `cargo fmt` and `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`: clean.
- Not yet seen on screen; the game's tests against it after its circuit
  is laid again for the speed.

## 2026-11-11 — Checks ridden at a hover bike's speed, bends that ask for the drift

### Implementation

- A hover bike grips across as hard as takes it flat out round a bend 170
  m round (`HOVER_GRIP`), no tighter: at 540 km/h a tighter bend asks to
  lift, or to drift (+30 %). At first it gripped as its speed squared
  would have it, and a lap of Puerto Neón was flat out all the way round.
- The circuits' checks ride each circuit with its own bikes
  (`BikeTuning::on`): the pack (`ride_pack`, `pack_race`), the start, and
  the flat-out lap. Its pilot looks as far ahead as it goes in 0.3 s
  (18 m at the least), steers the softer the faster past a superbike's
  speeds (the square root), and, on a bike that hovers, drifts in a bend
  it turns hard into and lifts at full lock; it counts as off the road
  only rough ground (a bridge's or a tunnel's paved shoulders, and all
  round a tube, are road).
- The checkpoints' spacing is as much wider as the bikes are faster.
- The ways off a skyway, where they are apart from it, have paved
  shoulders as it has, not grass.

### Verification

- `cargo test`: 682 passed; `cargo fmt` and `cargo clippy --all-targets
  -- -D warnings` clean. The game's tests against it: 240 passed.
