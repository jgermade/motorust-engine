# Costa Bits: arches of a theme's own, the narrow road, the voxels' colours, a neon sea, the tunnel, and falls by difficulty

## User request

From the game's Costa Bits (germadev/motorust#38):

- The theme's own arches (Meshy's) at the line and the checkpoints, in
  place of the engine's gantries.
- Under the overhang, the narrow lane showed a centre line and the asphalt
  past its walls: the road must narrow with them; its outer edge in a
  colour that stands out.
- The voxels of the ground mostly brown: greys, greens, oranges too.
- A light version of the 80s' neon grid over the sea.
- The tunnel's face and lining clash with the rest of the look.
- Falls from rubbing other riders or the walls: more tolerant the easier
  the race, the present behaviour as very hard.

## Implementation

- `world/scenery.rs`: `Gantry::drawn` (true unless a file says): a gantry
  not drawn is still a checkpoint (or the line) for the race, for a theme to
  draw an arch of its own there; `road::spawn_gantries` and `draw_signs`
  leave it out.
- `world/road.rs`: `narrowed` brings a ribbon's points in where the walls
  close in: the asphalt to `half_width_at`, its middle squeezed and the
  metre along each edge moved in as it is (the edge lines keep their width),
  the curbs moved in as far but never past the walls; no centre dashes
  where narrowed. On an arcade road, the low parapet red and white band by
  band.
- `world/terrain.rs`: `rock_stratum`: the voxels' rock in layers 2.5 m
  thick wandering ±3 m, each a hue of six (warm and cool greys, ochre,
  orange, red sandstone, pale limestone), the hues shifting from one
  stretch of cliff to the next (patches 90 m across), as light as before;
  green moss on level ledges in places.
- `world/sea.rs`, `world/mod.rs`: under a Synthwave sky over a sea, a neon
  grid on the water (`grid_mesh`: lines 0.5 m wide every 36 m, 1.8 km round
  the circuit, additive and unlit; the land hides it, the fog fades it).
- `world/structures.rs`: drawn as voxels, a tunnel's rock is painted per
  block as the voxels' rock is (`voxel_tunnel`) and nothing of it is
  textured (`voxel_tunnel_materials`: flat colours).
- `settings.rs`: `Difficulty::fall_tolerance` (easy 2.0, medium 1.6, hard
  1.3, very hard 1.0); `bike/crash.rs`: `Body::tolerance`, `rider_falls`
  and `wall_knocks_down` take it; `bike/mod.rs`: a player's (the bikes that
  feel hits) from the settings, the rivals' 1.
