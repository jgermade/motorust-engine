# Costa Bits: arches of a theme's own, the narrow road, the voxels' colours, a neon sea, the tunnel, and falls by difficulty

## User request

From the game's Costa Bits (germadev/motorust#38):

- The theme's own arches (Meshy's) at the line and the checkpoints, in
  place of the engine's gantries.
- Under the overhang, the narrow lane showed a centre line and the asphalt
  past its walls: the road must narrow with them; its outer edge in a
  colour that stands out.
- The voxels of the ground mostly brown: greys, greens, oranges too.
- A light version of the 80s' neon grid over the sea.
- The tunnel's face and lining clash with the rest of the look.
- Falls from rubbing other riders or the walls: more tolerant the easier
  the race, the present behaviour as very hard.

## Implementation

- `world/scenery.rs`: `Gantry::drawn` (true unless a file says): a gantry
  not drawn is still a checkpoint (or the line) for the race, for a theme to
  draw an arch of its own there; `road::spawn_gantries` and `draw_signs`
  leave it out.
- `world/road.rs`: `narrowed` brings a ribbon's points in where the walls
  close in: the asphalt to `half_width_at`, its middle squeezed and the
  metre along each edge moved in as it is (the edge lines keep their width),
  the curbs moved in as far but never past the walls; no centre dashes
  where narrowed. On an arcade road, the low parapet red and white band by
  band.
- `world/terrain.rs`: `rock_stratum`: the voxels' rock in layers 2.5 m
  thick wandering ±3 m, each a hue of six (warm and cool greys, ochre,
  orange, red sandstone, pale limestone), the hues shifting from one
  stretch of cliff to the next (patches 90 m across), as light as before;
  green moss on level ledges in places.
- `world/sea.rs`, `world/mod.rs`: under a Synthwave sky over a sea, a neon
  grid on the water (`grid_mesh`: lines 0.5 m wide every 36 m, 1.8 km round
  the circuit, additive and unlit; the land hides it, the fog fades it).
- `world/structures.rs`: drawn as voxels, a tunnel's rock is painted per
  block as the voxels' rock is (`voxel_tunnel`) and nothing of it is
  textured (`voxel_tunnel_materials`: flat colours).
- `settings.rs`: `Difficulty::fall_tolerance` (easy 2.0, medium 1.6, hard
  1.3, very hard 1.0); `bike/crash.rs`: `Body::tolerance`, `rider_falls`
  and `wall_knocks_down` take it; `bike/mod.rs`: a player's (the bikes that
  feel hits) from the settings, the rivals' 1.

## Verification

- `cargo fmt --check`, `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean;
  `cargo test`: 670 passed, 0 failed, 3 ignored (new: a gantry's `drawn`,
  an easier race's tolerance).
- Seen in the game (lavapipe, Costa Bits): the narrow lane without a centre
  line, its asphalt within its walls; the cliffs in layers of greys,
  ochres and oranges, green ledges; the neon grid on the sea; the tunnel's
  lining and frame in flat colours.

## User request

- A graphics option with the shadows soft as on low, but drawn as far as
  on high.

## Implementation

- `settings.rs`: `ShadowQuality::Soft`, offered between high and medium
  ("Difusas y lejanas" / "Soft, far"); `world/detail.rs`: `Level::Soft`,
  four cascades out to 250 m as high, each 1024 texels as low: soft all
  the way out, lighter to draw than high. With the performance forced, the
  ladder lowers it as any other (medium, then low).
- `options.rs`: its name.

## Verification

- `cargo test`: the levels' test checks soft reaches as far as high with
  low's maps.

## User request

- The lines flicker (aliasing): space them out, make them wider, and have
  them face the bike.

## Implementation

- `world/sea.rs`: lines 60 m apart (36), 1.2 m wide and wider the further
  from the road (`GRID_WIDENING`, 8 mm a metre), in pieces 15 m long, each
  turned about its line towards the nearest of the road (an eye 12 m over
  the sea, `GRID_EYE`) the further out it is (`GRID_TURN`: flat to 80 m,
  facing it wholly from 400 m), so that seen from the road it is not edge
  on, thinner than a pixel; crisp to 400 m, gone at 1000 m, 1100 m round
  (further, they piled up into a dark band at the horizon).

## Implementation

## User request

- The pieces turned to the road look horrible, the worst version, and they
  don't turn to the bike.

## Implementation

- `world/sea.rs`, `world/grid.wgsl`: the grid drawn by a shader of its own
  (`GridMaterial`) on the water outside the lap (`grid_mesh`: strips 20 m
  deep, each cut where it crosses the lap, 1200 m round): each pixel works
  out how far it is from the nearest line in pixels, from how far apart the
  lines are on the screen there (`fwidth`), so the lines are 2 pixels wide
  near and far, smoothed at their edges, and face the eye wherever it is;
  faded where a cell is only a few pixels on the screen (where they would
  crowd and shimmer) and from 350 to 1100 m from the camera. Lines 60 m
  apart, dark indigo. The quads turned to the road are gone.

## Verification

- `cargo test` and clippy; seen in the game: thin, steady lines on the open
  sea, none on the bay.

## User request

- Why do the lines stop short of the horizon?

## Implementation

- `world/sea.rs`: the grid's water 5 km round the circuit (in strips 40 m
  deep), its lines fading from 2 to 4.5 km from the camera: drawn by the
  shader, they thin out of themselves where the cells grow small on the
  screen, so they can run out towards the horizon without crowding.

## User request

- The lines still don't reach the horizon (it had not been checked: the
  screenshots of the sea had come out black); would raising the grid help?

## Implementation

- Raising it would not: every level plane meets the same horizon, and a few
  metres up at 7 km moves its edge a fraction of a pixel. The gap was the
  shader fading the lines out where the cells grew small on the screen.
- `world/grid.wgsl`: there, instead of fading them, as much of the pixel as
  the lines would cover on average (up to 30 %), so that they run on to the
  horizon as a haze of their colour; `world/sea.rs`: the grid's water 7 km
  round the circuit (as far as the sea), fading only from 6 to 7 km.

## Verification

- Seen (lavapipe, from the coast highway as the circuit loads, looking out
  to sea): the lines run on into a thin indigo haze at the horizon, no gap.

## User request

- Make the lines parallel, as in an 80s picture (lines across level with
  the horizon, the others running away to a vanishing point ahead).

## Implementation

- `world/grid.wgsl`: the grid squared to the view: its coordinates taken
  across the eye's heading (about the eye) and along it (from a fixed
  point), the eye's heading level from the view's forward axis; so the
  lines across lie level with the horizon and the others run away to the
  vanishing point ahead, turning with the eye, and going on, the lines
  across come towards it.

## Verification

- Seen (lavapipe, looking out to sea): level lines across and lines running
  to the point ahead, on to the horizon.

## User request

- Turning with the bike, the grid looks bad: better still. Could where it is
  drawn be shifted so that its cut-off top reaches the horizon? And rather
  than all over the sea, only on the water under the sun.

## Implementation

- `world/grid.wgsl`: the grid still on the water again, squared to the sun:
  one set of lines running out to it, the other across (level with the
  horizon seen towards the sun, as in the 80s pictures); only on a band of
  sea from the circuit's middle out to the sun, 1.4 km wide, fading over
  400 m at its sides and behind the middle (`GridMaterial::sun`, `band`,
  from the sky's sun); where the lines crowd far off, up to 55 % of the pixel
  in their colour, so that they run on into a band of it at the horizon.
  Shifting the grid towards the horizon would mean bending it about the eye,
  and it would then slide under it as the bike goes on.

## Verification

- Seen (lavapipe, looking out over the sea from the coast highway): the
  lines still, running out towards the sun, on to the horizon.
