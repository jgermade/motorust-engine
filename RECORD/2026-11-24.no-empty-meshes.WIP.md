# No Empty Meshes of the Roads and Structures

## 1. The Allocator's "Use-after-free" Errors in the Menus (2026-11-24)

**Date:** 2026-11-24. **Branch:** `claude/no-empty-meshes`, from `main` at `b89a7f8` (v0.0.31).

### 1.1 User Request
- Seen while working on the road blur: a game logged Bevy's `slab_allocator` "Use-after-free: attempted to copy element data for an unallocated key", 14 or 46 times, a few seconds after it started, in the menus. Offered to look at it apart; the user: "ok, dale".

### 1.2 What Was Found
- Bevy's mesh allocator (`bevy_render` 0.19, `MeshAllocator::allocate_meshes`) makes no room for a mesh with no vertices, but still copies its vertices and indices in, and logs that error for each: two lines a mesh. Harmless, but each is an empty mesh made and spawned for nothing.
- A scratch probe in the game (not committed) logged each mesh added with no vertices and what draws it: 23 of them, as the menus prepared two circuits in the background, each at the world's origin under the circuit's root, in white vertex-coloured materials and one in a game's magenta kerb glow (`road.rs`'s strips): pieces of the roads and structures (`road::road_pieces`, `structures::structure_pieces`) for kinds a circuit has none of.

### 1.3 Implementation Details
- `world/mod.rs` (`prepare`) and `world/preview.rs`: the roads' and structures' pieces with no vertices are left out once gathered, before they are spawned.
- New check `no_piece_of_a_circuit_is_left_empty_on` (in `world::tests`, run on the demo by `no_piece_of_the_demo_is_left_empty` and on a game's circuits by its checks): every circuit prepared has pieces, none empty.

### 1.4 Verification & Testing
- The check on a game's circuits fails without the fix (an empty piece on its toy store's circuit first) and passes with it.
- The game with this engine under a `[patch]`, twice 20 s in the menus: no error logged (46 each time before).
- `cargo test` (708 passed), `cargo fmt --all --check`, `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean.
