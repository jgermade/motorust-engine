# The Shaders Compiled on the Grid, and the Props Lighter Far Away

## 1. The Warm-Up, and Levels of Detail for the Prop Packs (2026-11-24)

**Date:** 2026-11-24. **Branch:** `claude/pipeline-warm-up`, from `main` at `d8a10a5` (v0.0.30). Worked on with a game beside it under a `[patch]`.

### 1.1 User Requests
- On a base M4 Mac mini a game's circuit had shown one-off hitches of 300–400 ms (its 1 % low at 27.8 fps). Three next steps were offered: confirm them with two circuits in a row, compile the pipelines before the start, and levels of detail for the props of the game's heaviest circuit (Puerto Neón, its city round the lap); the user: "hagamos las tres, tú decides el orden".

### 1.2 What Was Found
- Two circuits in a row with a scratch probe (not committed) logging every frame over 50 ms and every new pipeline: no hitch in the measured laps (the 1 % low of that circuit with one player at 63.6 fps). The only stalls were while each race was set up: the frame the circuit is built in (0.8–2.3 s), then 100–680 ms while 15 to 63 pipelines compiled at once, as Bevy compiles those of what a camera first sees.
- The game's packs of props made with Meshy have 23,000 to 116,000 triangles each (1,000–4,000 a piece), and Puerto Neón places more than a thousand of its city's around a lap of 7 km, all drawn whole however far, in every shadow cascade too: its shadows at medium (fewer cascades) ran 23 % faster.

### 1.3 Implementation Details
- **`src/camera/warm_up.rs`** (`WarmUpPlugin`, added by the camera's plugin): as a race is set up (`OnEnter(GameState::Race)`, after the split cameras) the riders' cameras get Bevy's `NoCpuCulling`, so everything of the circuit is queued for them and its pipelines compile on the grid (the GPU's own culling keeps the drawing as it was). A system in the render world counts the pipelines and those waiting (`Pipelines`, two atomics shared by both worlds); `WarmUp::step` ends it once at least 8 frames have gone by and for 0.3 s none has been waiting nor a new one come, or after 6 s, each frame counting for at most 0.1 s (the frame the circuit is built in takes seconds); then the cameras cull again, and it logs how long it took.
- **The start lights** (`race/lights.rs`, `run_lights`) wait for it: until `WarmUp::done`, no light comes on and the bikes stay held. Out of a race (or with no `WarmUp`, in tests) it is done.
- **`lod::simplify_textured`**: the edge collapse of `lod::simplify` (quadric error metrics, the vertices welded by position), but each corner left keeps its own attributes of the mesh as made (texture coordinates, the second ones a city's windows are drawn with, normals; `Work::corners`, `Work::textured`, `pick`), each triangle with corners of its own; so a piece made lighter is drawn with the same material, sharp edges and all.
- **`prop_pack`**: each `Piece` gets two lighter meshes as its pack loads (`lighter`: a quarter and a twelfth of its triangles, at least 48 and 24; one that would not be lighter is the full one), built on the cores (`terrain::on_cores`). `Piece::drawn` gives a theme the bundle to spawn a piece with: its mesh and `PropLevels`. `dress_props` (PostUpdate, as the trees are dressed) draws each prop in view with the mesh its distance from the nearest camera calls for: lighter from 4 and from 10 times its size (as it stands, scaled), with `lod::level_at`'s hysteresis. `PropPacks::load_for` logs how long each pack took.
- **The setting** `Settings::lighter_far_props` (on by default; files without it read it as on), in Options > Graphics > Advanced under the lighter far trees ("Objetos lejanos más ligeros" / "Lighter far props"), reset with the rest; in the performance log (`lighter_far_props=`).
- **The benchmark**: `Scenario::far_props` (on in the base), and `1p-whole-props` and `2p-whole-props`: 19 a lap with one player and 7 with two.

### 1.4 Verification & Testing
- New tests: `it_is_over_once_nothing_has_waited_for_a_moment_after_a_few_frames`, `it_takes_its_fewest_frames_even_with_nothing_to_compile`, `a_long_frame_counts_for_little`, `it_gives_up_waiting_after_the_longest`, `out_of_a_race_it_is_over`, `the_cameras_see_everything_until_it_is_over` (an app: the chase camera without culling once the race is set up, culling again once it is over); `a_lighter_textured_mesh_keeps_the_texture_of_each_corner_as_made` (a sphere, each triangle its own corners, two sets of coordinates: a quarter and a twentieth of its triangles, every corner one of those made, its normals out, within its box).
