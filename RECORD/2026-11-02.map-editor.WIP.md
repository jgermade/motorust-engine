# The map editor

## 1. A first editor: the lap's control points, from above, saved into the circuit's file

**Date:** 2026-11-02. **Branch:** `ccr-703edef7-3dm1sq`.

### User request
"Can you start working on a map editor?" (the game's `ROADMAP.md`, section 3, step 5: the editor in the engine, inside the game's own world, so that what is edited is seen as it will be raced; the limits the tests check shown as it edits, shared with the tests).

### Implementation
- **`src/editor/limits.rs`:** the limits of a lap, shared with the tests: `MIN_RADIUS_IN_WALLS` (2.5), `MAX_GRADE` (0.13), `too_tight`, `too_steep`, `curvature` and `grade` as `Track` works them out; `Preview`, a lap laid out from its control points and height profile as `Track::lap` does (the same curve, spacing and profile), with where it breaks the limits, its tightest bend and steepest grade: cheap enough to work out again at each move of a point. `track::tests::curves_are_drivable_on` and `slopes_and_banking_stay_rideable_on` use them. (`track::profile_height` made public for it.)
- **`src/editor/file.rs`:** `write_control_points`: a circuit's file with its `control_points` as the editor has them, the rest as it was: each point kept written over its old self (comments round it kept), each removed taken out (with its line if nothing else is on it), each added on a line of its own after the point before it, to the centimetre; refused, changing nothing, if the file's list is not as the editor read it. Comments and strings are skipped when finding the list and its points. A check for a game's circuits: `every_circuit_s_points_write_back_as_they_were_on` (unchanged, a file is as it was; every point moved a metre, it reads back so, its heights as they were).
- **`src/editor/view.rs`:** the editor's camera over a point on the ground (`View`: turned, tilted, near or far, panned along the screen's axes), the point nearest the cursor on the screen, where the cursor's ray meets a level, the point halfway to the next.
- **`src/editor/mod.rs`:** `EditorPlugin` (added by `app()`; nothing unless asked for): `--edit [circuit]` or `MOTORUST_EDIT`, in a desktop development build only (where circuits are read from the disk and written there); the circuit by its file's name or its place. A new `GameState::Editor`, entered at start-up. `Edit`: the points, the one picked, undo and redo, and whether it differs from the file; never fewer than four points. Systems: the view steered with the keys and the mouse, the camera placed by it, a point picked (within 16 px) and dragged over the level it is at, the keys (Tab, N/Insert, Delete/Backspace, Ctrl+Z/Y, Ctrl+S, Page Up/Down, Home), the lap drawn with gizmos (green, red where it breaks a limit and across the road there, its walls, the points as spheres as big on the screen at any distance, the one picked yellow), and a text in the corner (the circuit, the lap's length, its tightest bend and steepest grade against the limits, the point picked, the keys, what it last said). The fog pushed off to 4–6 km. Its gizmos drawn on the layer seen from above (`realm::OVERVIEW_LAYER`), so that the interface's camera does not draw them too, flattened, and in front of what is there. Saved, the circuits' watch reads the file again (`circuits::reload`) and the world builds the circuit anew; the editor reads it again then, unless it has changes of its own not saved. In the menus' background work, the editor counts as a race (nothing prepared meanwhile).
- **`src/content.rs`:** `write`: a file of the packs written on the disk where the first pack listing it has it (a development build's).
- **`src/menu.rs`:** `GameState::Editor`. **`src/world/mod.rs`:** no circuits prepared in the background in the editor.
- **`README.md`:** the map editor, how to start it and its keys.

### Verification
- New tests: `editor::limits` (a wide flat circle keeps the limits, one too small for its walls is too tight all round, a steep climb is marked where it is, the limits are the tests', the preview is the lap the game lays: the demo circuit's points and curvatures as `Track`'s), `editor::file` (numbers to the centimetre; unchanged points leave the file as it was; a moved point written in its place with its comments; a removed point goes with its line or leaves its comment; added points on lines of their own; what the file has not refused; the demo circuit written back), `editor::view` (the camera looks at its focus from above; panning moves along the screen; zoom and tilt within limits; the nearest point picked; a ray meets a level; a point between), `editor` (the flag; a circuit by its name or place; adding and removing points; undo and redo; Tab round the points).
- `cargo test`: 607 passed, 2 ignored. `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`: clean. `cargo fmt --all --check`: clean.
- In the editor (Xvfb with lavapipe, about 2 FPS, driven with xdotool): Bahía GP from above with its points and lap; a point dragged 100 m east, the lap through it worked out again, its bend there 18.7 m against 28.8, marked red; Ctrl+S wrote that one line of `assets/circuits/bahia.ron` (`(433.46, -3.69)` in place of `(345.0, -10.0)`), the circuits were read again and the world built the new road (the change then undone in git). Seen once: the interface's camera drew the gizmos too, flattened in the middle of the screen (fixed with the layer).
- Left: the heights (the profile), banking and stretches, branches, what stands along the road, entering and leaving it from the menus, and the crest check (it needs the bike's speed), as the roadmap says.

## 2. The height profile and the banking; entered from the tracks menu

**Date:** 2026-11-02. **Branch:** `ccr-703edef7-3dm1sq`.

### User request
"Go on with the heights and the banking. I was thinking of entering the editor from the track select menu, only when the game is started in development mode (`make up`)."

### Implementation
- **`src/editor/profile.rs`** (new): the profile's marks (`Mark`: along, height, which of the file's it was) and the stretches of banking (`Span`: from, to, factor, raised, grip), read from the layout and written as the files write them (`3.4028235e38` for a stretch to the end, `raised` and `grip` only when not as usual); a mark moved between its neighbours (2 m apart), added halfway to the next; a stretch added in the gap where the cursor is (60 m, factor 1.5), split in two, its ends moved within their room (an end joined to the next stretch's start moves it too; never shorter than 4 m); `items`, a list as the file is to have it, unchanged items keeping their text. `Panel`: where the panel along the bottom of the screen is (30 % of it), the lap across it, heights up it (never flatter than 20 m), the band of banking under it, and the mapping both ways.
- **`src/editor/file.rs`:** `write_list`, any list of a circuit's file written as the editor has it (`write_control_points` now one of its uses): a kept item that did not change keeps its text (comments inside it too); a list on one line written again an item a line; an item added after the last of a list gives it a comma; a list the file has not added after another (the banking after the profile).
- **`src/editor/limits.rs`:** the bump off the middle of the road (`bump_off_middle`, `MAX_BUMP` 0.6 g at 30 m/s, 4 m off), shared with `track::tests::the_banking_puts_no_bumps_off_the_middle_of_the_road_on`; `Preview::of_track`: the lap as the game lays it whole, its heights lifted where it banks raised, its bank and its bumps. `Track::bank_at` (new).
- **`src/editor/mod.rs`:** `Edit` holds the points, the marks and the stretches, and what is picked (`Selection`: a point, a mark, a stretch, nothing); undo and redo of all of it. The plugin is there in every desktop development build (`available()`); `--edit` only starts in it. A change lays the lap out at once from its points and profile, and whole a quarter of a second after it settles (`lay_out_whole`: a `Track` from the lists as edited, the branches and narrows as read; the lists leaked, a few kilobytes at each settled change, for as long as the game runs; a lap that cannot be laid out is told, not a crash). The mouse picks and drags a point on the map, a mark or a stretch's end on the panel (the panel held still while dragging); the keys add, split, remove, change a stretch's factor, raising and grip; Esc goes back to the tracks (twice with changes not saved). Drawn: on the map the walls at the bank's heights, bumps orange across the road, the marks as posts along the lap, the stretch picked along both walls, the cursor's place along the lap; on the panel (a camera of its own over the world's and under the interface, seeing only its layer, `PanelGizmos`, over a dark background) a grid every 10 m and 100 m, the road's height (red where too steep, orange ticks where bumpy), the marks, the stretches as bars as high as their factor (grey level, blue less, green more, orange raised or gripping harder), the bank, and the cursor. Saved, the three lists are written (`write_lap`). Leaving puts the fog and the gizmos back as they were.
- **`src/track_select.rs`:** E opens the editor on the track chosen, in a development build; the legend says so (keyboard).
- **`README.md`:** the map editor, as it is now.

### Verification
- New tests: `editor::profile` (marks and stretches written as the files write them and read back; a mark moves between its neighbours; one added goes halfway; a new stretch fills its gap; a stretch splits in halves; a stretch's ends move within their room and with the next; the panel maps both ways), `editor::file` (a list on one line written an item a line; an item after the last gives it its comma; a list the file has not added after another; an unchanged item keeps its text and comments), `editor::limits` (the whole lap as the game lays it has its banking and no bumps), `editor` (marks added and taken out, never under two; banking added, split, changed and taken out, and undone; a lap edited everywhere is read back as edited: `a_lap_edited_is_read_back_as_edited_on`, for a game's circuits too).
- `cargo test`: 622 passed, 2 ignored. `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`: clean. `cargo fmt --all --check`: clean.
- In the game (Xvfb with lavapipe, about 2 FPS, driven with xdotool): the tracks menu's legend with "E Editor"; E opened the editor on Bahía GP with its panel (its 20 marks, the bank's line), Esc went back to the tracks menu, its fog as before. With `--edit`: a mark dragged from 13 m to 18.5 m up made a grade of 14.5 %, red on the panel and on the map; B with the cursor at 335 m on the band added a stretch, `]` three times and T made it a factor of 1.8, raised; Ctrl+S wrote the mark's line and a new `banking` list after the profile into `assets/circuits/bahia.ron`, read again and built anew (undone in git afterwards). Seen and fixed: E, which also turns the view, still held from the menu turned it half round (now not turning until let go); the panel hard to read over the world (now a dark background).

## 3. A terrain mode, as a theme park game's terrain editor

**Date:** 2026-11-02. **Branch:** `ccr-703edef7-3dm1sq`.

### User request
"Let's go on with the editor: I'd like something like how Jurassic World Evolution's terrain editor works."

### Implementation
- **`src/world/sculpt.rs`** (new): the ground shaped by hand over the land the scenery makes: `Sculpt`, heights over a regular grid (2 m, `CELL`), interpolated (nothing off it); `Brush` (a tool, a radius and a strength, all of it over its inner half and fading out to its edge) and `Tool` (raise, lower, smooth toward the ground's average round each point, flatten to a level, restore toward nothing), applied for a time step with the ground as it stands; written to a file and read back (`MRSC`, a version, the grid's origin, cell and size, a height a point in centimetres, little-endian: about 480 KB for Bahía GP). `companion`: `circuits/NAME.terrain` beside `circuits/NAME.ron`.
- **`src/world/scenery.rs`:** `Scenery::sculpt` (not in the file: `#[serde(skip)]`), filled from the companion by `Circuits::parse` (`src/circuits/mod.rs`, `sculpt_of`) where a pack lists one.
- **`src/world/terrain.rs`:** `Shaper::height_at` adds the shaping, all of it away from the roads and from just past their walls (`SCULPT_REACH`, `sculpt_reach`: the road keeps the ground beside it); none indoors (the floor). In a development build the ground's meshes are kept in the main world too, to be shaped in place. `FINE_MARGIN` public.
- **`src/world/mod.rs`:** `BuiltTerrains`, the terrain of each circuit put in the world whole, in a development build, for the editor to see how high the ground is.
- **`src/circuits/reload.rs`:** the companions watched with the circuits' files (a circuit whose companion changed is new, `companions_changed`) and read again with them.
- **`src/content.rs`:** `list_beside` (a file listed in a pack's `pack.txt` after another, the pack's list read again), `write_bytes`; the packs' lists' cache module-wide.
- **`src/editor/terrain.rs`** (new): the terrain mode (`Shaping`: on or off, the shaping edited and as built, the brush, strokes to undo, the cursor on the ground): M switches it; 1 to 5 the tool; [ ] or Shift+wheel its size, , . or Ctrl+wheel its strength (the wheel zooms otherwise). The cursor's ray marched to the ground as it now stands (`ray_to_ground`). While the left button is held, a stroke (remembered to undo, its level where it began; a grid over the track's box and its fine margin made at the first stroke on a circuit with none) applied each frame, and the ground's meshes of the circuit's world shaped in place round the brush: each vertex as built plus the change, as much of it as its distance from the roads lets (worked out once a vertex), facing as the ground now does. The brush drawn on the ground (its edge and its inner ring, coloured by its tool). Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo strokes in this mode. Saving writes the companion (listing it first), and the circuit's file if the lap changed too; the circuit is read again and built anew with the shaping. The lap's panel and picking are off in this mode; the text says the mode, the tools, the brush.
- **`README.md`:** the terrain mode.

### Verification
- New tests: `world::sculpt` (a companion beside its circuit; raising lifts the middle most and nothing past the edge, and lowering; nothing off the grid; flattening brings a slope to the level; smoothing takes a spike down and restoring takes it all; a file read back as written to the centimetre, and nonsense or a short file refused; a brush within its limits), `editor::terrain` (a ray meets the ground where it is, and not upward or from under it; a stroke undone and done again), `circuits::reload` (a circuit whose companion changed is new).
- `cargo test`: 632 passed, 2 ignored. `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`: clean. `cargo fmt --all --check`: clean.
- In the editor (Xvfb with lavapipe, about 2 FPS, driven with xdotool): M, the brush grown to 48 m, shown on the ground; raising held 12 s by the lap raised a hill beside the road, the road and its walls left as they were, shaped in place as it went; lowering held over the sea; Ctrl+S wrote `assets/circuits/bahia.terrain` (477,658 bytes) and listed it in `assets/pack.txt` after the circuit, the circuit read again and built anew: the hill now rock where it is steep, no trees on it (the change then undone: the file removed, the list as it was).

## 4. Painting the ground, the circuit built anew as it is edited, and what stands by the road

**Date:** 2026-11-03. **Branch:** `ccr-703edef7-3dm1sq`.

### User request
"Go on with the three things you propose" (painting the ground's kinds; the trees and the grass seen as the ground is edited, not only once saved; placing what stands by the road).

### Implementation
- **Painting** (`src/world/sculpt.rs`): `Paint` (as the land makes it, grass, sand, rock), a byte a point of the grid (`Sculpt::paint`, `paint_at`: the nearest point's), `Tool::Paint` (the points the brush covers past a third of its weight painted); the file's version 2 (the paint after the heights; version 1 still read, with no paint). `src/world/terrain.rs`: a painted point's ground is what it was painted as, in the meshes (`Site::painted`, honoured by `look`) and for what stands on the ground (`Terrain::ground`, which the trees and the grass go by). In the editor's terrain mode 6 to 9 paint; while painting, the paint round the brush is drawn as crosses of its colour.
- **The circuit built anew as it is edited** (`src/editor/terrain.rs`, `rebuild_live`): a moment (0.8 s) after a stroke or a change in the roadside mode, new circuits made from their files as read, the one edited from its file's text as the editor has it (what stands by the road written into it in memory) and its ground shaped as the editor has it (`circuits::Edited`, `Circuits::parse_shaped`); the world told it was read again. `src/world/mod.rs`: in the editor the world being replaced is not taken away but marked `Superseded` and kept in sight, the new one built without waiting (as in the tracks menu), out of sight, its look at the start not shown, and put in sight in its place once whole (its trees and grass), the old one taken away then. The editor then shapes from the new terrain (its `built` the shaping it was built with); shaped further meanwhile, it is built anew again. Brushing shapes the meshes of the world in sight. The editor's text says while it builds; `follow_reloads` leaves a live rebuild alone.
- **What stands by the road** (`src/editor/roadside.rs`, new): `Kind` (arch, billboard, chevron, brake marker, grandstand), `Placed` (where it stands, its text as the file is to have it, which of the file's it was), `Roadside` (read from the circuit and its file's text, undo and redo, add, remove, flip, move, write). The mouse picks one by its post and drags it along the road (`Track::nearest` on the lap's own road: how far along, and which side); N adds one of the kind chosen (1 to 5) where the cursor is (a chevron pointing round the bend there); X, Delete, Tab. Drawn as posts (an arch also as a bar across the road), sized to the view. `src/editor/file.rs`: `list_items` (a list's items' texts), `set_field` (one field of an item set, the rest as it was, comments too), `has_list`; `write_list` leaves a list nothing changed in as it was (a list on one line was written again an item a line).
- **`src/editor/mod.rs`:** `Mode` (lap, terrain, roadside; M goes round them); the lap's panel, picking and keys only on the lap; saving writes what stands by the road too; leaving or going to another circuit asks first if it changed.
- **`README.md`:** painting, the live rebuild, the roadside mode.

### Verification
- New tests: `world::sculpt` (painting covers the brush and unpainting takes it off; a file of the first version reads with no paint; paint read back as written), `editor::file` (a field of an item set, the rest as it was; inside another not the item's own; added where missing), `editor::roadside` (what stands by the road read back as placed: every item moved and turned, one of each kind added, one taken out, for a game's circuits too: `what_stands_by_the_road_is_read_back_as_placed_on`; a new one written as the files write them; changes undone and done again).
- `cargo test`: 638 passed, 2 ignored. `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`: clean. `cargo fmt --all --check`: clean.
- In the editor (Xvfb with lavapipe, 1–2 FPS, xdotool): sand painted beside the road, the paint drawn round the brush as it went; 0.8 s after the stroke the circuit built anew with it, not saved, the old world in sight meanwhile: a patch of sand on the ground, no trees on it. A hill raised and built anew: rock on its steep sides, the trees gone from them. In the roadside mode a billboard added on the start straight (107 m, right); saved, `assets/circuits/bahia.ron` had one line more in its billboards (undone afterwards). Seen and fixed: a list on one line rewritten though unchanged; the posts too small to see from above; the lap's "Picked" line in the other modes.

## 5. Buttons for every action, with their keys and legends; the whole legend in a window

**Date:** 2026-11-03. **Branch:** `ccr-703edef7-3dm1sq`.

### User request
"The editor's menu is not at all intuitive: it should have buttons for the actions, as well as hotkeys. I'd put each action's legend beside its button, and a global legend in a modal that opens with Tab."

### Implementation
- **`src/editor/ui.rs`** (new): `Action`, what the editor does; `BINDINGS`, the one table of which key (with Ctrl or Shift, or another key for the same) does which action in which mode (`Scope`), what it is called in Spanish and in English (the game's language), and whether it has a button; `MOUSE`, what the mouse and the view's keys do, for the legend. `read_keys` turns the keys pressed into actions (with the legend open, only closing it); the buttons (`ActionButton`) pressed give theirs (`press_buttons`); both into `Actions`, which the editor's systems carry out alike and which is cleared each frame. The mouse over the interface (`EditorUi`, `Actions::over_ui`) is not the map's: no picking, dragging, brushing, panning or zooming through it. The interface (`spawn_ui`): a bar along the top (the circuit, marked if not saved; the three modes as tabs; Save, Undo, Redo, Help, Leave, the last and next circuit, the whole circuit in view; the lap's figures under them); a column down the left for each mode, shown in it, with a button for each of its actions (its key on a badge, what it does beside it), what is picked or how the brush is, and what the editor last said; the legend's window, every key of every mode and the mouse's, in groups, opened and closed with Tab (or its button; Esc closes it too). `style_ui` lights what is chosen (the mode, the brush's tool, what N adds, the help) and what the mouse is over; `show_texts` keeps the texts up to date.
- **`src/editor/mod.rs`:** `act` (in place of `edit_keys`) carries out the actions: those of the lap, undoing and redoing what the mode in use edits (the terrain's strokes and what stands by the road built anew after), saving, the circuits, leaving; `switch_mode` from the tabs and M. "Next" and "last" are Space and Shift+Space now (Tab is the legend's). From the buttons, banking goes where the lap is nearest the middle of the view, and what stands by the road is added there (no cursor on the map then). The old block of text is gone: the lap's figures go to the top bar (`figures`), what is picked to the column (`picked_line`). `src/editor/terrain.rs` and `roadside.rs` take their tools, sizes and kinds from the actions.
- **`README.md`:** the interface.

### Verification
- New tests: `editor::ui` (no two bindings of a mode share a key; keys written short as on a badge, in either language; every tool and every kind has its key and its names). `vector_art`'s check that every character shown is in the UI font caught a backspace symbol and a dot: written as words now.
- `cargo test`: 641 passed, 2 ignored. `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`: clean. `cargo fmt --all --check`: clean.
- In the editor (Xvfb with lavapipe, 1–2 FPS, xdotool): the top bar, the lap's column with its buttons and their keys, the panel under them; M to the terrain (its tools, the one chosen lit) and to the roadside mode; Tab opened the legend's window over it all and Tab closed it. Clicked: the "Terrain" tab switched the mode, "Paint sand" chose the tool (lit, nothing painted on the map under it), "Help" opened the window. Seen and fixed: the legend's keys and labels did not line up (a column of their own now); the mouse's keys in Spanish in an English interface.
