# 2026-11-10 — The clouds: no pyramid in every sky, none cut off

On `ccr-e7021c37-ulgd1v`.

## User request

A cloud (a screenshot of it: a heap rising to a point, like a pyramid) was
seen over and over, on every track.

## Context

The sky paints ten cloud shapes at startup (`cloud_image`) and deals them
round its 22 clouds in turn (`index % CLOUD_SHAPES`), in the same places on
every track. Painted out to look at (a test, ignored, that writes them to
files), two of the ten were towering cumulus (`CloudKind::Towering`, shapes
1 and 6), their towers stacked three or four high, each narrower than the one
below: a pyramid with a spire, five clouds of the 22. Shapes 1 and 9 were
also cut off along the top of their images, and 2 and 8 at their right
side: straight edges in the sky. The test that no cloud is cut off only
looked at the border pixels, which `clear_borders` clears whatever the
cloud.

## Implementation

- `src/world/sky.rs`:
  - Shape 6 is a cumulus: one towering shape of the ten, three clouds of the
    22.
  - Towers broad and rounded, each bulging out of the one below (radius 0.14
    to 0.2 of the image's height, rising by 0.55 to 0.8 of it), two of them;
    the towering heap wider (0.6 to 0.7 either side) and less tall (1.15).
  - `fit_in_image`: every cloud's heaps shrunk about their lowest base, and
    moved across, if their puffs reach past the image's edges less
    `CLOUD_MARGIN` (0.07 of its height), before they are painted.
  - Each track's ring of clouds turned by its own angle (`clouds_turn`: the
    golden angle times the track's index) when its light is put on
    (`light_the_sky`), so the same heap is not over the same corner of
    every track.
  - Tests: `clouds_are_heaps_on_soft_uneven_bases` checks the pixels 4 in
    from the edges too (nearly clear); new,
    `each_track_has_its_clouds_elsewhere_in_the_sky` (the first ten tracks'
    turns well apart, one towering shape); `dump_cloud_images` (ignored)
    writes the ten images, to look at.

## Verification

- The ten images painted out after the change: none cut off; no pyramid; the
  towering one a broad rounded heap.
- `cargo fmt --all --check`, `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`:
  clean. `cargo test`: 668 passed, 0 failed, 3 ignored (built with no debug
  information: the disk was full).
